## What P3 delivers

- Observer CRUD: add (from placement Enter/Готово), rename, duplicate, delete — each with custom Undo.
- Panel list + card actions: Войти, Обновить наблюдателя, Новый наблюдатель здесь, Дублировать, Удалить, Переименовать.
- Saved markers via DisplayConduit when the panel is open.
- Enter from selected record (`_AWEnter` / double-click).
- Walk start pose is not rewritten unless **Обновить наблюдателя**.
- Unit scale: foot coords scale once with geometry; `H` stays physical metres; Undo of scale.
- Named Views: `_AWSaveView` with unique name; refuse overwrite unless `ReplaceExisting`.
- Import/insert does not replace current repository (P0D + isolation checks).
